Brand identity guide · Michigan pilot

The road is the message.

Interstate 75 runs from Detroit to the tip of the Lower Peninsula and keeps going south through five more states. The mark is built from that road — three colors, one number, zero ambiguity about what it stands for.

02 · Construction

Every part earns its place

Click a spec to see exactly what it controls. Nothing in this mark is decorative — the map, the star, the stripes and the numeral are all load-bearing.

Michigan mapFull state silhouette, both peninsulas, solid #00274C. No county lines, no lakes cut out — recognizable at a glance, nothing to lose at small sizes.
Detroit starPositioned at roughly 88.5% across and 87% down the map's bounding box — Metro Detroit, southeast corner. #B31942 fill, thin white keyline so it reads on the dark map.
Wordmark"DRIVE" and "FOR" stacked, heavy italic condensed caps, #00274C. Cap height sets the module every other element is measured against.
Motion stripesThree tapered bars — maize / red / maize — launched from the right edge of the R with a constant keyline gap, cut parallel to the diagonal of the 7. Top bar longest, none touch the numeral.
75Spans both wordmark lines, #B31942, heavy italic. It is the single largest shape in the mark — every other element is sized to stay secondary to it.
Tagline"A GRASSROOTS MI INITIATIVE" — #00274C, wide letterspacing, roughly a quarter the cap height of the wordmark. Establishes Grassroots MI as the parent without competing with the mark.

07 · Motion

The stripes accelerate

0–0.4s

The top stripe extends first, from zero width to full — the road catching up to the letter.

0.12–0.5s

The red center stripe follows a beat later, same easing, same direction.

0.24–0.6s

The bottom stripe completes the sequence. All three settle; nothing loops on its own.

Rule

Use this once, on load — a dashboard header, a video intro. Never as a hover toy or a background loop.

09 · Rules

Keep it this way

Hover each card to see what breaks the mark.

✗

Don't rotate or mirror it. The star and stripes only make sense at true north.

✗

Don't stretch or squash the mark to fill a space.

✗

Don't recolor the mark outside the four approved colors — no green, no purple, no brand-of-the-month palette.

✗

No gradients, glows, or drop shadows. The mark is flat, always.

✗

Don't let red fill the background. Red marks the destination, never the field.

✗

Don't add an outline, stroke, or badge ring around the mark.
