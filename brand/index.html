Brand identity guide · Michigan pilot

The road is the message.

Interstate 75 runs from Detroit to the top of the Lower Peninsula and keeps going south through five more states. The mark is built from that road — three colours, one number, and no ambiguity about what it stands for.

02 — Construction

Every part earns its place

Hover or tap a spec to isolate it in the mark. Nothing here is decorative — the map, the star, the stripes and the numeral are all load-bearing.

Michigan mapFull state silhouette, both peninsulas, solid #00274C. No county lines and no lake cut-outs — recognisable at a glance, nothing to lose at small sizes.
Detroit starPlaced at roughly 88.5% across and 87% down the map's bounding box — Metro Detroit, southeast corner. #B31942 with a thin white keyline so it holds against the blue.
WordmarkDRIVE and FOR stacked in heavy italic condensed caps. Its cap height is the module every other element is measured against.
Motion stripesThree tapered bars — maize, red, maize — launched from the right edge of the R at one constant keyline gap, then cut parallel to the diagonal of the 7. The top bar is longest; none of them touch the numeral.
The 75Spans both wordmark lines in #B31942. It is the single largest shape in the mark, and everything else is sized to stay secondary to it.
TaglineA GRASSROOTS MI INITIATIVE, wide-tracked at roughly a quarter of the wordmark's cap height. It credits the parent organisation without competing with the mark.

07 — Motion

The stripes accelerate

The mark has one motion signature, and it is already built into the logo: three bars launching from the R, one after another, toward the 75.

0–0.7s

The top bar extends from zero to full width — the road catching up to the letter.

0.12s

The red centre bar follows a beat later, same easing, same direction.

0.24s

The bottom bar completes the sequence. All three settle and stay.

Rule

Use it once, on load — a site header, a video intro, a dashboard. Never as a hover toy or an endless background loop.

09 — Rules

Keep it this way

Hover each card to see what breaks the mark.

✗

Don't rotate or mirror it. The star and the stripes only make sense at true north.

✗

Don't stretch or squash the mark to fill a space.

✗

Don't recolour it outside the four approved values. No green, no purple, no colour of the month.

✗

No gradients, glows or drop shadows. The mark is flat, always.

✗

Don't let red fill the background. Red marks the destination, never the field.

✗

Don't add an outline, a keyline or a badge ring around the mark.
